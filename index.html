The data

10,000 digits from the MNIST dataset, split into 8,500 for training and 1,500 held back for testing. Training images were randomly rotated, rescaled and thickened so the model copes with mouse and finger drawing.

The model

A multilayer perceptron: 784 inputs, hidden layers of 128 and 64 ReLU units, 10 softmax outputs. Trained in scikit-learn with Adam and early stopping, then compressed to 8-bit weights so the whole model is about 110 KB.

In the browser

The forward pass is about forty lines of plain JavaScript: three matrix multiplications and a softmax. No ML library, no server, and it runs on every stroke.
